# Art licenses

All art in this folder is by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). Credit is optional and appreciated.

- city/*.webp: isometric renders (Blender, scripts/blender/render_city.py) of Kenney's City Kit (Commercial 2.1, Industrial, Roads, Suburban), Car Kit and Nature Kit (CC0 3D models). The compound buildings (courtyards, podiums, wings, solar halls, yards, terraces, tower clusters) are assembled from pieces of those kits in the same script
- fx/*.webp: Kenney Particle Pack (CC0)

The interface font, Plus Jakarta Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1), ships through the Fontsource package.
