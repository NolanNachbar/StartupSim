# Player walkthrough: the checker that plays the game

The other checkers grade pixels, behaviour and code. This one grades whether a stranger understands the game. It exists
because Nolan found problems that none of those checkers could: a map that did not make sense, labels that meant
nothing, a business setup that could not describe a gym, questions that were the same for every business, no sign of
when you have launched, and no explanation of how the business earns money.

## How it works

The checker plays as a first-time founder with a specific business in mind, through the real UI, and writes down every
moment where they would be lost. It does not know the rules in advance. It reads only what is on screen.

Personas to run (one per pass; vary them):
1. A gym ("a small strength-training gym": Health and wellness, Physical location).
2. A cafe or restaurant (Food and drink, Physical location).
3. A reminder app for clinics (Health and wellness, Software or app).
4. A handmade candle brand (Retail and e-commerce, Physical product).
5. Free text that matches nothing on the list (a wedding photography service).

## What it must answer, with screenshots

1. After each screen: what is this, and what am I supposed to do? Quote the text that says so, or say there is none.
2. Does every label, number and button mean something to someone who has never seen the game? List any that do not.
3. Is the wording right for this business? Flag any word that belongs to a different kind of business (an "app", a
   "prototype", a "landing page", "customers" where the business has patients or members).
4. Can the player tell how the business makes money, and what marketing and hiring do? Quote where it is explained.
5. Can the player tell when the business is ready, launched or open? Quote it.
6. After "Wait a week", is there an overview of what happened? Is it understandable?
7. Do the interview questions and customer answers fit this business, or could they be pasted into any business?
8. Is there any moment the player gets stuck, or can lose progress by accident?
9. Would a stranger finish a run and want to play again? What is the weakest moment?

## Verdict

Fail if any of items 1 to 7 has a confusing or wrong-fit moment. Return a numbered list, ranked by how likely a
new player is to quit at that moment, with the screenshot and the text involved.
